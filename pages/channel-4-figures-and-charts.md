# Channel 4 · Figures and charts

[⬅ Back to README](../README.md) · [◀ Channel 3 · Tables](channel-3-tables.md) · [Channel 5 · Equations ▶](channel-5-equations.md)

**What it is:** graphical encodings of quantity, decoded through an axis.

**What a pooled page vector is not required to keep:** the mapping from position to value.

* [NeurIPS 2024] **CharXiv: Charting Gaps in Realistic Chart Understanding in Multimodal LLMs** [[DOI](https://doi.org/10.52202/079017-3609)]
